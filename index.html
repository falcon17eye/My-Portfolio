About Me

Who I Am

I'm Mohammad Ibrahim, a Game Designer passionate about crafting intriguing Player Experiences. From Systems design to UI/UX — I approach every aspect of Design with a sharp, analytical mind and a keen eye for intentional details.

My design philosophy centers on "Intentionality" to build a game where all its aspects are pointing towards a specific, intentional experience, making all the instruments play the same note.
