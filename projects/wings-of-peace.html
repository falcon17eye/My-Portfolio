Wings of Peace

Playing as a homing pigeon, you must reunite the two kingdoms before war ruins everything.

Role Team Manager & Game Designer
Genre Narrative Adventure
Context Egypt Game Jam 2024 — ITI
Tags Story / Singleplayer

Overview

"Wings of Peace" is a captivating narrative-driven game where players assume the role of a courageous messenger pigeon caught in the midst of a war-torn world. As the avian protagonist, your mission is to deliver vital messages between two warring nations, ultimately striving to bring about reconciliation and peace.

Set in a beautifully rendered world ravaged by conflict, the game offers a unique perspective on the consequences of war and the power of communication in fostering understanding and harmony. With its emphasis on storytelling and emotional depth, "Wings of Peace" immerses players in a poignant tale of hope, sacrifice, and the enduring spirit of compassion.

Cover Art

Gameplay Features

  • Free Flight Navigation: Full 3D flight across a war-torn world — navigate through skies, ruins, and no-man's-land to reach your destination
  • Message Delivery System: Interact with points of interest to deliver letters that gradually shift the tide of war
  • Narrative Focus: Every delivery advances a story of hope — the game's mechanics directly serve its emotional message
  • Atmospheric World: A beautifully rendered environment where the conflict is visible on the land below you

Controls

  • Flight: WASD — Navigate the skies
  • Elevate: Shift — Go higher
  • Land: Ctrl — Go lower
  • Interact: E — Land on points of interest

My Role & Contributions

  • Team Manager: Led a team of 8 across programming, art, animation, music, and design under game jam conditions
  • Game Designer: Designed the core narrative loop — linking flight mechanics to story progression so each delivery felt emotionally weighted
  • Defined the pacing of message deliveries to build tension and resolution as the player works toward peace
  • Coordinated across disciplines (programming, 3D modeling, animation, music) to maintain a unified vision under tight constraints

Design Challenges

The central design challenge was making the act of flying and delivering feel emotionally resonant — not just mechanically functional. A pigeon is a fragile, humble creature, and the game needed to make the player feel the weight of what they carry. Balancing free-form flight exploration with a clear narrative direction, all under a jam deadline with a large team, required careful scope management and constant communication.
